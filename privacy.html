Table Tycoon

Privacy Policy

Effective 23 October 2026.

Table Tycoon does not collect personal data. There are no accounts, no ads, no tracking and no third-party analytics.

What stays on your device

Your scenario progress, medals and settings are stored only on your device. We never receive them. Deleting the app deletes them.

Purchases

In-app purchases are handled by Apple through the App Store. We don't receive your payment details or Apple ID. The app keeps a record on your device of which unlocks you own so it can work offline, and checks it with the App Store when you restore purchases.

Children

Table Tycoon collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new effective date. Questions: open an issue.
