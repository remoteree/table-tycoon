Questions

How do I seat a party?

Tap a party in the line at the bottom of the screen, then tap a free table big enough for them.

What are the circles that pop up during service?

Live problems: fires, spills, a broken dish machine, a VIP or a complaint. Tap one before its ring runs out. Ignored problems cost money and stars.

How do I build?

Tap Build, pick a tab and an item, tap the floor to move it, rotate it if you need to, then tap the check mark to place it.

I bought something on another device.

Open Shop or Settings and tap Restore Purchases. Purchases are shared with your Family Sharing group.

There's no sound.

Check the music and sound settings in the game and your phone's volume.
